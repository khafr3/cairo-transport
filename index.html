Greedy — Traffic Signal Optimization
Real-time signal timing based on congestion ratios. Green time proportional to traffic demand.
Time Period:
⚙️ Algorithm Details
1
Compute congestion ratio = flow / capacity for each road
2
Sort roads at intersection by ratio (descending)
3
Allocate green time proportionally: green = (ratio / total) × 120s
T
Time: O(I × R log R)
I = intersections, R = roads per intersection. Greedy: fast but suboptimal globally
⚠️
Suboptimal Cases
Greedy ignores downstream cascade effects — optimizing one intersection may worsen adjacent ones
